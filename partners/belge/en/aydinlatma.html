7. Retention periods

Identity / passport number, date of birth, telephone, address, tax number, company address and IBAN or account number are stored encrypted. Only an authorized administrator can access this information; every access is logged.

  • Financial records and agreement information: For the periods set by the Turkish Commercial Code and Tax Procedure Law from the end of the relationship (10 years).
  • Image of identity document (only if separately requested where necessary):
  • If the application is rejected, deleted within 30 days of rejection.
  • If the partnership ends, deleted when the legal retention period expires.
  • Representative application details: Stored encrypted. If the application is rejected, deleted within 30 days of rejection; if accepted, kept for the duration of the relationship.
  • Login and transaction security records: 2 years.
  • Data whose period has expired is deleted, destroyed or anonymized.

8. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your data is processed.
  • Request information if it has been processed.
  • Learn whether it is used in accordance with its purpose.
  • Know the persons to whom it has been transferred.
  • Request correction if incomplete or incorrect.
  • Request deletion or destruction.
  • Request that correction, deletion and destruction be notified to those to whom it was transferred.
  • Object to an adverse result arising from analysis by automated systems.
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage.

You can send your requests to info@winway.com.tr or winway@hs01.kep.tr. Requests are concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the GDPR Notice also applies.
