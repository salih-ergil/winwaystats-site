Article 2 — Definitions

In this agreement:

  • App: The Company's WinwayStats mobile application and web version.
  • The App is a football prediction experience and behavioural awareness application that involves no real money.
  • The virtual unit in the App ("coin") cannot be purchased, sold, transferred or converted into money.
  • Program: The Winway Partners affiliate program.
  • Panel: The Winway Partners panel on winwaystats.com and the summary screen in the App.
  • User: A person registered with the App.
  • Linked User: A User linked to the Partner under Article 6.
  • Subscription: A paid Winway Plus package obtained by a User through Google Play, the App Store or another payment channel offered by the Company.
  • Packages given as ranking prizes or granted free of charge by the Company are not Subscriptions.
  • Referred Revenue: The amount remaining to the Company from each successful Subscription payment of a Linked User after deducting value added tax and similar taxes and the fee of the payment channel (Google, Apple or a payment institution).
  • It is shown in the Panel as "Revenue you referred".
  • It refers only to the amount from the Partner's Linked Users, not to the Company's total revenue.
  • Commission: The share of Referred Revenue calculated for the Partner under Article 7.
  • Membership Payment: The fixed amount paid to the Partner once for each Linked User meeting the conditions in Article 8.
  • Partner Representative: A person who supports partners under Annex 1 and receives a share of the Referred Revenue of linked partners.
  • Holding Period: The time a record stays "pending" in the Panel (Article 9).
  • Withdrawable Balance: The total of amounts whose Holding Period has ended and which have not been cancelled or paid.
  • Minimum Limit: The lowest Withdrawable Balance required for a payment (Annex 2).
  • Payment Day: The 18th of each month.
  • If that day is a public holiday or not a banking day, the first banking day after it.
  • Campaign, Link, Code: The promotional name created by the Partner in the Panel and its personal link and promotional code.

Article 3 — Subject of the Agreement

3.1. The subject of the agreement is:

  • The Partner's promotion of the App to its own community in accordance with the rules in Article 12.
  • The Company's payment of Commission on Referred Revenue from Users coming through the Partner, and of Membership Payments for Users who meet the conditions.

3.2. The Program gives the Partner no in-app advantage. Coin, point, ranking and prize rules are not affected by the Program.

Article 4 — Participation, Application and Approval

4.1. Only the following may apply to the Program:

  • Natural persons aged 18 or over with the capacity to act.
  • Duly represented legal persons.

4.2. Applications are made through the Panel with an App account. Identity details are collected only from Program applicants; they are not requested for App membership. The application requires:

  • Full name.
  • Turkish ID number; for non-citizens, passport number and country.
  • Date of birth.
  • Address and telephone number.
  • Country of residence.
  • Partner type (individual / company). For a company: company name, tax office, tax number, MERSİS or registry number if any, and company address; the personal details belong to the authorized person.
  • Channel or account links and approximate number of followers.
  • Payout account and account holder name: IBAN in Türkiye; abroad, bank name, bank country, SWIFT / BIC code, IBAN or account number and, where required in that country, the local bank code.

4.3. The Company reviews the application. The Company may reject an application without giving reasons. Participation in the Program is not a right. No image of an identity document is requested in the application; the Company may request one where legislation or a specific review so requires, and will inform the Partner separately in that case.

4.4. By submitting the application, the Partner is deemed to accept that the information given is accurate and complete. The Partner is responsible for delays and losses in payments or financial documents arising from incorrect or incomplete information. The Partner reports any change to its details within 15 days through the Panel or to info@winway.com.tr.

4.5. The payout account (IBAN) must be in the name of the Partner or, if the Partner is a legal person, in its own name. No payment is made to an account in another person's name.

4.6. A person may hold only one partner account in the Program. The same person may also be a Partner Representative (Annex 1).

Article 5 — Account Security

5.1. Two-step verification is mandatory to access the Panel. The Partner does not share its verification tool or password with anyone.

5.2. Actions taken from the Partner account are attributed to the Partner. The Partner immediately notifies the Company of any suspected unauthorized use.

5.3. Changing the payout account (IBAN) requires a two-step verification code. No payment is made to the new account for 7 days after the change. The change is notified to the Partner's registered email address.

5.4. When the IBAN or personal details change, an email is sent to the Partner. If the Partner did not make the change, it can suspend its account with the button in the email. No actions can be taken on a suspended account, the changed payout account is cancelled and only the Company can remove the lock.

Article 6 — Linking Users to the Partner

6.1. A User is linked to the Partner in one of the following ways:

  • (a) Installing the App and registering through the Partner's Link.
  • (b) Entering the Partner's Code during registration.
  • (c) Entering the Code once in the App settings within 7 days after registration.

6.2. A User is linked to only one Partner and the linked Partner cannot be changed. If a User used the Links of more than one Partner before registering, the last Link used before registration or the Code entered at registration prevails.

6.3. In the following cases the User is not linked to the Partner and no payment is made for the User:

  • The Partner's own App account.
  • Accounts opened from the Partner's own device.
  • Second and subsequent accounts opened from the same device.

6.4. Users registered before the Program are not linked to a Partner.

6.5. The Company's records prevail in determining which Partner a Link belongs to and whom a User is linked to (Article 25).

6.6. The Partner sees only the following details of Linked Users:

  • The permanent account number assigned by the Company.
  • Registration date and country.
  • Subscription package and status.
  • Referred Revenue and Commission amounts.

The User's name, username, contact details and activity in the App are not shown to the Partner.

Article 7 — Commission

7.1. The Partner earns Commission on each successful Subscription payment of a Linked User at the rate of Referred Revenue set out in Annex 2.

7.2. The Commission rate:

  • Is initially 10%.
  • May be set higher by the Company specifically for the Partner or according to the tiers in Annex 2.
  • May in no case exceed 20%.

7.3. Commission is entered in the Panel on the date of payment:

  • Each month for a monthly package.
  • Each week for a weekly package.
  • In one go on the payment date for 6-month and annual packages.

7.4. Commission continues without time limit for as long as the Linked User maintains the Subscription.

  • If the User cancels the Subscription or it ends, no new Commission is entered.
  • If the User subscribes again later, Commission is entered again.

7.5. Packages given as ranking prizes or granted free of charge by the Company generate no Commission. They are also not included in subscriber counts in the Panel.

7.6. Rate changes apply only to payments after the change. Commission entered in the Panel is not reversed except under Articles 9 and 17.

7.7. For tiered rates (Annex 2):

  • The tier is determined by the average of the last (3 months).
  • If the average falls below the threshold, the tier drops only after it stays below for 2 consecutive months.
  • The Company announces changes to the tier table through the Panel and by email at least 30 days in advance.

7.8. Referred Revenue is calculated on the basis of:

  • The amount, tax rate and fee rate reported by the payment channel.
  • For payments not in Turkish lira, the rate closest to the payment date from the daily exchange rate source used by the Company.

Article 8 — Membership Payment

8.1. The Partner earns a Membership Payment once, in the amount set out in Annex 2, for each Linked User meeting all the following conditions. The initial amount is TRY 20 per User. The conditions, within 90 days of registration, are:

  • (a) Having entered the App on at least 68 of the 90 days and having spent at least 2 minutes in total in the App on those days.
  • (b) Having made a coupon on at least 3 different days in each of three 30-day periods.
  • (c) Not having deleted the account and not having the account suspended before the 90 days end.
  • (d) Not falling under Article 6.3 (one account per device; excluding the Partner's own account and device).

8.2. The Membership Payment is independent of the Subscription Commission. If the Linked User also subscribes, the Commission continues separately.

8.3. In one calendar month, a Partner receives Membership Payments for at most the number of Users set out in Annex 2. This number is initially 500.

8.4. For a User meeting the conditions, the Membership Payment is added directly to the Withdrawable Balance when the 90th day is completed.

8.5. If the Company's system detects an unusual situation:

  • That Partner's Membership Payments are held until the Partner Representative or the Company completes a review.
  • No payment is made for Users found to involve abuse after the review.

Examples of unusual situations:

  • Many Users appearing active but almost none subscribing.
  • Users' login times and usage patterns being excessively similar.
  • More than one account opened from the same device.

Article 9 — Holding Period, Refunds and Corrections

9.1. Commission and the Partner Representative share stay "pending" for 30 days from the date they are entered in the Panel, then move to the Withdrawable Balance.

9.2. In case of a refund:

  • If the payment channel refunds a Subscription payment, the pending record for that payment is cancelled.
  • If the record has moved to the Withdrawable Balance or has been paid, the same amount is deducted from the Partner's balance as a negative record.

9.3. If a technical error, duplicate record or miscalculation is found, the Company corrects the record with a new correction record.

  • Records in the Panel are not deleted. Corrections appear on a separate line.
  • Overpaid amounts are set off against later payments. If set-off is not possible, the Partner returns the amount within 30 days of the Company's notice.

Article 10 — Payment

10.1. On each Payment Day the Company prepares a payment list for partners who:

  • (a) Have reached the Minimum Limit (initially TRY 1,000) in Withdrawable Balance.
  • (b) Have a registered payout account that is more than 7 days old.
  • (c) Have active partner status (not suspended).
  • (d) Are eligible for domestic payment under Article 10.9.

10.2. A balance below the Minimum Limit carries over to the next month. Pending amounts are not included in the payment.

10.3. Payments are made:

  • In Turkish lira.
  • By bank transfer or EFT to the Partner's registered payout account. International payments, when opened, are made by international bank transfer (SWIFT).
  • Within a reasonable time after the Company approves the payment list.

10.4. Who bears bank charges is announced in the Panel before payment.

10.5. Selecting a currency other than TRY in the Panel is for display only. Payments are made in TRY.

10.6. If a payment fails because the Partner's payment details are incorrect, the amount stays in the balance and is paid on the Payment Day after the details are corrected.

10.7. During a review under Article 17, the Company may postpone payment of the relevant amounts until the review is completed.

10.8. Making a payment does not mean accepting that the Partner has complied with this agreement.

10.9. For domestic payment, the Partner must have declared that it is resident in Türkiye or a taxpayer in Türkiye, and must have an account opened in Türkiye.

  • Commission and Membership Payments of a Partner not meeting these conditions are entered in the Panel and accumulate.
  • Payment is made after the date on which the Company considers international payments legally and financially appropriate and announces this in the Panel.
  • No interest or other consideration is paid for this waiting period.

Article 11 — Tax and Financial Obligations

11.1. The Partner is responsible for its own tax and reporting obligations relating to income earned under this agreement.

11.2. If legislation in force obliges the Company to withhold tax, tax is withheld; the Panel shows the gross amount, deduction and net amount separately.

11.3. To the extent permitted by legislation, the Company issues an expense voucher (gider pusulası) for payments made to a Partner participating as an individual. The Partner's full name, Turkish ID number and address are used for this purpose.

11.4. A Partner participating as a company issues an invoice to the Company for payments made.

11.5. The Company may request other documents required by legislation (invoice, self-employment receipt, etc.) from the Partner. If a required document is not provided, payment is held until the document arrives.

Article 12 — Promotion Rules

12.1. The Partner promotes the App accurately and without misleading anyone. The App must be presented as a football prediction experience without real money and an awareness application that lets people see their own prediction habits.

12.2. The Partner may not:

  • (a) Present the App as a betting site, betting app or a platform that earns real money.
  • (b) Use expressions such as "earn money", "guaranteed winnings", "guaranteed coupon", "convert coins into money" and the like.
  • (c) Promise Users any advantage in the App, extra coins, prizes or any benefit on behalf of the Company.
  • (d) Give Users money, gifts or other benefits for registering or using the App. This includes running a draw in its own name and on its own account.
  • (e) Open fake accounts, have them opened, or generate registrations or usage with automated tools.
  • (f) Send unsolicited bulk messages (spam).
  • (g) Write fake comments or reviews or have them written.
  • (h) Promote the App in content or channels aimed at people under 18.
  • (i) Speak on behalf of the Company, make contracts on its behalf or imply being its employee or official representative.
  • (j) Use the App brand as a keyword in paid search ads or acquire a domain, account or app name resembling the brand. This may be done with the Company's written permission.
  • (k) Act contrary to the rules of Google Play, the App Store and the platform where the post is made.

12.3. In posts promoting the App, the Partner states that this is a collaboration in accordance with advertising legislation in force (e.g. "#ad", "#collaboration").

12.4. The Partner may use images (banners) offered by the Company in the Panel without changing them. This article also applies to changed images or images it has prepared itself.

12.5. The Company may ask for a post it finds contrary to the rules to be removed. The Partner complies within 48 hours at the latest.

Article 13 — Relationship with App Rules

13.1. The Program does not change the App's coin, point, ranking and prize rules. Being a partner gives no advantage in the App.

13.2. The Partner's own App account is subject to the same rules as other Users.

Article 17 — Abuse

17.1. The following are abuse:

  • The prohibitions in Article 12.2.
  • Registrations and usage that are fake, automated or created through incentives (money, gifts).
  • The same person benefiting from the Program with more than one account.
  • Transactions intended to deceive the payment channel.
  • Any attempt to circumvent the Company's registration and measurement systems.

17.2. In case of suspicion, the Company may:

  • Hold the relevant amounts during the review.
  • Ask the Partner for an explanation.

17.3. If abuse is established, the Company may:

  • (a) Cancel Commission and Membership Payments obtained through abuse.
  • (b) Set off these amounts already paid against later payments or reclaim them from the Partner.
  • (c) Suspend the partnership or terminate the agreement immediately under Article 18.4.
  • (d) Claim compensation for its loss.

Article 18 — Suspension and Termination

18.1. The Company may suspend the partnership in the following cases:

  • Suspected breach of this agreement.
  • A request from the payment channel or competent authorities.
  • Inability to verify the Partner's information.

18.2. During suspension:

  • (a) No new Commission or Membership Payment is entered for the Partner.
  • (b) The unpaid balance (pending and withdrawable) is frozen.
  • (c) At the end of the review, the Company releases the frozen amounts or cancels them under Article 17.3.

18.3. Either party may terminate the agreement without giving reasons on 15 days' written notice (including email).

18.4. The Company may terminate the agreement immediately by notice in case of breach of Article 12 or Article 17.

18.5. On termination:

  • (a) Amounts entered in the Panel up to the termination date and not involving abuse are paid according to the Holding Period and Minimum Limit rules. A final balance below the Minimum Limit is also paid on the first Payment Day after termination.
  • (b) No Commission is entered for the Partner for payments made after termination.
  • (c) Linked Users are not linked to another Partner.
  • (d) In immediate termination under Article 18.4, Article 17.3 applies.

Article 19 — Deletion of the Account

19.1. The Partner may request deletion of its App account. If there is an unpaid balance, there are two options:

  • The account is not deleted until the balance is paid.
  • The account is deleted if the Partner expressly confirms in the Panel that it waives its balance.

19.2. Even if the account is deleted, financial records (amounts, dates, payment records) are retained with personal data separated for the retention period set by legislation.

Annex 1 — Partner Representative Annex

This annex applies, in addition to the Affiliate Agreement, to persons approved by the Company as Partner Representatives. Provisions of the Affiliate Agreement not otherwise specified in this annex also apply to the Partner Representative.

A1.0 — Application

To become a Partner Representative, a separate application is made through the Panel. No new account is needed; the application is linked to the partner record. The application asks the person to introduce themselves, any experience, how they want to support partners and the time available; no CV file is requested. The Company reviews the application and may reject it without giving reasons. The role does not begin until the partner application is approved. If the partner application is rejected, the representative application is rejected automatically.

A1.1 — Duties

The Partner Representative:

  • (a) Finds suitable partner candidates and invites them to the Program.
  • (b) Helps partners with applying and using the Panel.
  • (c) Supports partners linked to it or assigned by the Company on promotion, rules and earnings calculations.
  • (d) Reviews unusual situations in the "To review" screen of the Panel within 7 days at the latest. For each one, it either decides "genuine" or forwards it to the Company with a reasoned note.
  • (e) Helps partners comply with the promotion rules in Article 12. It reports breaches it sees to the Company.

A1.2 — Linking Partners to the Representative

  1. A partner applying through a Partner Representative's invitation link is linked to that representative when the application is approved.
  2. The Company automatically assigns a Partner Representative to a partner applying on its own. Assignment is made only to representatives considered active under A1.5, to the one with the fewest partners. Assignment is made among representatives who speak the partner's language. If none is available, the Company supports the partner until one is found.
  3. Each partner has one Partner Representative. The Company may change the link. The partner may request a change of representative from the Panel.

A1.3 — Representative Share

  1. The Partner Representative receives a share of the Referred Revenue of each linked partner at the tiered rate in Annex 2. The initial rate is 2%. The share applies to every linked partner, whether linked by the representative's invitation or by automatic assignment.
  2. The representative share is not deducted from the partner's Commission. It does not reduce the partner's entitlement.
  3. The representative receives no share of Membership Payments.
  4. The tier is determined by the average number of active subscribers of the representative's partners in the last 3 months.
  5. A tier drop requires the average to stay below the threshold for 2 consecutive months.
  6. A share entered in the Panel is not reversed. Articles 9 and 17 are reserved.
  7. If a partner is moved from one representative to another, shares entered up to the transfer date stay with the old representative. Later shares are entered for the new representative.
  8. The holding period, minimum limit, payment day and tax provisions of the Affiliate Agreement also apply to the representative share.

A1.4 — Information It May See

The Partner Representative sees the following information about linked partners:

  • Name.
  • Channel.
  • Partner status.
  • Number of users brought and active subscribers.
  • Referred Revenue and its own share.

It does not see partners' identity, contact and bank details, or the account numbers and personal data of the users partners bring. In reviews it sees only summary indicators.

A1.5 — Activity

  1. The Company monitors the activity of representatives with the following indicators for the last 30 days:
  2. Bringing new partners.
  3. The rate of partners bringing registrations.
  4. The rate of newly assigned partners bringing their first registration in the first 14 days.
  5. Partners' monthly satisfaction score (1–5).
  6. Last login to the Panel.
  7. Completing reviews within 7 days.
  8. A representative is considered active if all the following are met:
  9. Having entered the Panel in the last 30 days.
  10. Having brought a new partner or at least half of its partners having brought registrations.
  11. A satisfaction score of 2 or above.
  12. No new partner is assigned to a passive representative.
  13. The partners of a representative passive for more than 60 days may be transferred to another representative by Company decision.

A1.6 — Prohibitions

The Partner Representative:

  • May not promise partners rates, payments or privileges on behalf of the Company.
  • May not ask partners for fees, shares or any other benefit.
  • May not conclude reviews falsely in favour of its own partners.

Breach of these prohibitions is abuse under Article 17.

A1.7 — Both Partner and Representative

A person may be both a partner and a Partner Representative. The two earnings are shown separately in the Panel and assessed together for payment. A representative may not be the representative of its own partner account.
