4. Transfers to third countries

Data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Because the data controller is in Türkiye, data is also accessed from Türkiye. There is no European Commission adequacy decision for Türkiye. The basis for the transfer will be stated in this notice once finalised; you can write to info@winway.com.tr for information. If your Partner Representative is in another country, your name, channel and performance summary may be viewed from that country.

5. Retention periods

  • Rejected applications: until dispute periods end.
  • Agreement and financial records: for the legal period after the relationship ends (10 years).
  • Login and security records: 2 years.
  • Commercial message consent: for the time needed as proof after consent is withdrawn.

6. Security

Identity / passport number, date of birth, telephone, address, tax number and bank account number are stored encrypted. Administrator access is logged.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to request its correction, erasure and restriction of processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect processing before withdrawal. You can send your requests to info@winway.com.tr. You also have the right to complain to the data protection supervisory authority in your country.

8. Automated decisions

Applications are assessed by an administrator. Abuse flags may arise automatically, but a person makes any decision with legal effect. The pre-check uses automated checks and an AI-assisted suggestion; the suggestion is not binding and an administrator makes the decision.
