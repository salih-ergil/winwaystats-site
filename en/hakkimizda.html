About Us

9 October 2026

WinwayStats

WinwayStats is a platform focused on awareness and behaviour tracking that helps users explore their betting behaviour through their own data.

The platform lets you clearly see under which conditions you change your decisions, how you behave after losing and winning streaks, your balance usage habits, and how your decisions vary at different times of day.

Our aim is not to make you bet more or to teach you to bet better. Our goal is to let you see your own decisions through a neutral mirror, without any outside guidance, and to support your self-control.

What You Can Do with WinwayStats

• Review your own data: See your past decision processes and behaviour as a whole.

• Track your balance management: Follow your balance usage rates and risk changes over time.

• Notice your emotional reactions: Discover how your decisions take shape after losing and winning streaks.

• Analyse timing: See on which days and at which hours your decision profile differs.

• Safe simulation: Analyse your own behaviour patterns with virtual coins, without any real-money risk.

Our Approach

WinwayStats's approach is to hold up a mirror to the user's behaviour rather than judge it.

The platform does not provide a psychological or medical diagnosis and does not make decisions on your behalf. It helps you understand your own data and develop your awareness.

No real-money betting takes place on WinwayStats; coins and points have no monetary value.
