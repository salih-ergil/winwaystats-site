Competition and Reward Rules

13 November 2026 (effective 1 December 2026)

1. Nature of the competition

The WinwayStats monthly ranking is a skill competition based on knowledge and decision quality, held on the accumulated result of football prediction decisions over a calendar month. The ranking is determined not by the outcome of a single slip but by the total of points won and lost across all slips in the month.

Participation is free. No paid subscription or any other payment is required to take part, earn points or receive a reward by meeting the conditions in these rules; coins cannot be bought. Every user receives a monthly budget of 50,000 ₵ on the 1st of each month.

Access to app features may differ depending on the membership package and on any trial or promotion period. However, the membership type does not change the monthly coin budget, how coupons are scored, the ranking and tie-break rules, the conditions for earning spendable points or the point values of rewards. A paid subscription does not grant extra coins, extra points, a points multiplier, ranking priority or any privilege when claiming rewards.

The conditions on account age, participation on different days, points, balance, ranking and Reward Periods apply in the same way to all participants, regardless of membership type. A paid subscription does not remove or ease any of these conditions; on its own it does not entitle anyone to a reward and does not guarantee winning one.

Coins and points have no monetary value; they cannot be sold, transferred or converted into cash or any other value. Points can only be used for the rewards defined in these rules.

The competition is organised solely by Winway Veri Analizi ve Yazılım Danışmanlık Hizmetleri Limited Şirketi ("WinwayStats"). Apple Inc. and Google LLC are not sponsors of this competition or its rewards and are in no way involved in them.

2. Points and ranking

Points are won or lost according to the result of each slip. Points depend on the outcome of the decision, not on the number of predictions.

A slip's points are calculated as follows:

• Winning slip: for a single, the match odds × 1.33. For a combo, the sum of the odds of the 3 highest-odds matches × 1.33; if the slip has fewer than 3 matches, the odds of all matches are added. Example: a single at 2.40 earns 3.19 points; a combo at 2.29 and 1.48 earns (2.29 + 1.48) × 1.33 = 5.01 points.

• Losing slip: the points the slip would have earned if it had won are divided by the total odds minus 1 and recorded as negative points. The total odds are the product of the odds of the matches on the slip. Example: a single at 1.50 earns +2.00 points if it wins and −4.00 if it loses; a single at 3.00 earns +3.99 if it wins and −2.00 if it loses. As a result, a loss on a low-odds slip and a win on a high-odds slip affect points more.

• Slip amount: full points require a slip amount of at least 500 ₵ (1% of the monthly budget). Below that, points decrease in proportion to the amount (a 250 ₵ slip earns half points). Amounts above 500 ₵ do not earn extra points. This rule also applies to the negative points of a losing slip.

• Odds used for points: points are calculated from the odds shown in the app at the moment the slip is played, which are the odds the slip is played at; later changes in a match's odds do not change the slip's points.

• Cancelled match and refund: a cancelled match on a combo is removed from the calculation and points are calculated with the remaining matches. A refunded slip and a slip that has not yet been settled earn 0 points.

• Repeated slip: a slip with the same predictions that is played again within 3 minutes of an earlier slip that earned points earns no points; it counts for coins only.

• Points are rounded to two decimal places. A slip's points are added to the month in which the slip was played. A month's point total can be negative.

The monthly ranking is based on the total points of that month and resets on the 1st of each month (Türkiye time). There is one ranking for all users; there is no separate ranking by country.

If points are equal, the user with the higher win rate for that month (the share of winning coupons among the coupons settled that month) ranks first; if that is also equal, the user who comes first in username order.

No reward transaction affects ranking points: receiving a reward does not change ranking points or past tables.

Basic ranking information and the user's own competition data can be viewed with every membership type. Access to other users' detailed coupon history, their pending coupons open to sharing and coupon analysis features may differ by membership package. These differences in access do not change how points are calculated, the ranking rules or the conditions for qualifying for rewards.

3. Eligibility for rewards

To take part in a month's rewards, all of the following must be met at the end of that month:

• The account must be at least 30 days old.

• Slips must have been placed on at least 14 different days in that month. A sustained decision process over the month is rewarded, not a short burst.

• The User Agreement and these rules must have been complied with. Points and rankings obtained through multiple accounts, automation, account sharing or cheating are void. Every reward request is reviewed before approval; a request with no violation is approved, a request with a violation is rejected and the related points are void.

Accounts that do not receive rewards: WinwayStats staff, test and review accounts. These accounts may appear in the ranking but do not receive rewards. Users in the partner programme can receive rewards under the same rules as other users.

4. Monthly package reward (PRO time)

Each month, among users who meet the conditions in section 3 and whose month-end balance is above 50,000 ₵, the users ranked in the top 20 by points who have collected at least 120 points that month receive in-app Winway Plus package access. These are promotional rewards separate from physical rewards.

• Places 1–13: 1 month PRO

• Places 14–20: 15 days PRO

For a user with an active package, the time won starts after the current package ends. The reward package is free; it cannot be converted into cash, coins or another reward, cannot be transferred, and is not subject to refund or withdrawal.

PRO access is open for the duration of the package reward; when it ends, FREE access applies. The end of the period does not change points already earned, the ranking or reward rights.

The package reward is credited to the account automatically after all slips of that month have been settled, and no later than the 8th of the following month.

The package reward applies in all countries and is independent of physical rewards; both a package and a physical reward can be won in the same month.

5. Spendable points

Separately from ranking points, each user has a spendable points balance. This balance is used only to request rewards in a Reward Period (section 6); it does not affect the ranking.

A month's points are added to spendable points at month end if: the conditions in section 3 are met, the month-end balance is at least 35,000 ₵ and at least 150 points were collected that month. The points of a month that does not meet this condition stay in the ranking, are not added to spendable points and do not carry over to the next month.

Spendable points decrease as they are used in reward requests. Unused spendable points expire at the end of the last Reward Period of the season in which they were earned (31 July); they do not carry over to the new season.

From the 2027-28 season, when spendable points can be used is determined by two tiers:

• A (Immediate): month-end balance of 50,000 ₵ or more, 200 points or more that month, and a top-20 place in the monthly ranking → the very next month's Reward Period (example: September points → October).

• B (Accumulation): month-end balance of 35,000 ₵ or more, 150 points or more that month, regardless of rank → not the next, but the following month's Reward Period (example: September points → November).

Tier A rewards success in both the ranking and keeping the balance in the same month; tier B rewards sustained regular success and patience.

2026-27 season transition rule: As this season has two Reward Periods, the A/B distinction is not applied; the points of every qualifying month can be used in the nearest Reward Period. Points from December 2026 to March 2027 are used in the April 2027 period, points from April to June 2027 in the July 2027 period. Points accumulated in October and November 2026 under the rule in force at the time (balance at least 40,000 ₵ and at least 100 points) count as spendable points and can be used in the April 2027 period; nobody's earned points are removed retroactively. The tier distinction starts with September 2027 points.

6. Reward Period and physical rewards

The Reward Period is the time during which rewards can be requested with spendable points. Rewards are gift vouchers, products and other rewards announced in the WinwayStats reward list. Money is not given as a reward; WinwayStats does not convert a reward into cash, coins or points and does not pay money instead of a reward. What the user does with a delivered reward afterwards is up to the user.

2026-27 season calendar:

• 1st Reward Period: requests 1–15 April 2027, delivery 16–30 April 2027.

• 2nd Reward Period: requests 1–15 July 2027, delivery 16–30 July 2027.

From the 2027-28 season, a Reward Period opens every month; the first is October 2027 (with September 2027 points). The days of the monthly period are announced before the season starts.

Reward list and point values: The reward list of each Reward Period and the point value of each reward are announced in the app and on winwaystats.com at least 20 days before the request period starts. The point value of rewards is announced together with the reward list.

Request order and stock: During the first 5 days of the request period, only the 20 users with the highest spendable points can make requests; from day 6, everyone who meets the conditions can. For transparency, these 20 users are shown by username in the app before the request period starts. Stock is limited for each reward. A user can request the same reward once per period; different rewards can be requested as far as spendable points allow. If a reward runs out of stock, a gift voucher of equal point value is given if the user accepts; otherwise the points are refunded.

Country: In the 2026-27 season, physical rewards are delivered only to delivery addresses in Türkiye. For users outside Türkiye, the package reward (section 4) continues unchanged. Spendable points of users outside Türkiye also accumulate and can be used when their country is opened for rewards; if it is not opened, they expire at season end under section 5. New countries are announced at least 20 days before opening.

Delivery: Rewards are sent by WinwayStats within the delivery period by courier, or by e-mail for gift vouchers. If a reward cannot be delivered, a second attempt is made before the period ends; if it still cannot be delivered, the points are refunded. Shipping and delivery costs are not charged to the user.

Information requested for delivery: Only from a user whose reward request has been approved, and only for delivery: name and surname, delivery address, phone number, a declaration of being over 18; an identity number where required by law. This information is not used for anything other than delivery and legal obligations; the retention period is stated in the Privacy Policy. If the user does not provide the information, the request is cancelled and the points are refunded.

Taxes and legal obligations: Taxes and similar legal obligations relating to rewards are fulfilled by WinwayStats; no fee of any kind is requested from the user.

The reward is delivered to the owner of the account that made the request; the right to request cannot be transferred to another account, and WinwayStats does not convert the reward into cash or another reward.

7. Changes to the rules and announcements

These rules apply to months after their publication date; the rule of a month that has already started is not changed within that month. Rewards for November 2026 and earlier months are given under the rules in force in those months.

Rule changes are announced in the app and on winwaystats.com before they take effect; for the reward list and point values, the period in section 6 applies.

The user accepts these rules together with the User Agreement. When the rules are updated, the user is asked once in the app to confirm them again.
