Delete account

You can delete your WinwayStats account in the app (Settings > Delete Account) or on this page.

  • After the request, the account waits for 30 days. If you sign in to the app during this time, the request is cancelled.
  • After 30 days your account and personal data (name, email, phone, date of birth, favourites, follows, poll votes) are permanently deleted.
  • Slip and virtual coin records are not deleted; their link to your account is removed and they are only used in anonymous statistics.

Sign in with your app account.
