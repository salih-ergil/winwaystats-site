2. Definitions

• Coin: The virtual unit used to create coupons in the Application, credited to the account as 50,000 on the 1st of each month. It is not money or a right with monetary value.

• Points: The virtual measure calculated from coupon results that determines the ranking.

• Coupon: A virtual prediction record made up of match outcomes the User selects using coins.

• Pisagol: Sample predictions shared in the Application by WinwayStats's statistical model.

• Personal Pisagol: A tool that the User sets up with leagues, teams and rules of their own choice, whose predictions only the User can see, and that creates sample predictions in the Application with its own separate virtual coin balance.

• Package (Winway Plus): A membership giving access, for a set period, to additional analysis tools and to the additional views stated in the package description. A package can be bought or can be given as a trial, promotion or reward.

3. Nature of the Service

The Application is a behavioural awareness platform offering football statistics, predictions with virtual coins, rankings and analysis tools that let the User see their own decision behaviour.

No real-money betting, games of chance or gambling take place in the Application; no money is deposited and no money is won. Coins and points cannot be converted into money, goods or any other value.

Odds shown in the Application come from independent data providers and are used only to calculate virtual points. WinwayStats is not a representative or partner of any betting company and does not direct the User to any betting site.

Statistics, odds and predictions (including Pisagol) are for information only; they are not betting, investment or financial advice, and their accuracy is not guaranteed. The User is solely responsible for any actions they take outside the Application based on this information.

The Application does not provide psychological, medical or therapeutic services, does not diagnose and does not make decisions on the User's behalf. If the User feels in difficulty, they can contact the support organisations shown in the Application.

6. Community Rules and Comments

Users can comment on matches and on the statistics on the team statistics and team comparison pages, reply to comments, like coupons and comments, and tag users with whom they have a follow relationship in match comments using @username. A statistics comment is linked to a statistic chosen by the User and is shown together with that statistic's value at the time of writing; these comments are for observations based on statistics and may not contain tips, claims of certain outcomes or coupon sharing. Users cannot delete their comments; they may request deletion, which is reviewed by a moderator. Users are asked to accept these rules before their first comment.

The following are prohibited in comments: insults, swearing, hate speech and discrimination; harassment, threats and bullying; sexual content; spam, advertising and sharing links; sharing the User's own or another person's personal information (phone, e-mail, address, etc.); promoting or referring to betting and gambling sites; any unlawful content.

Comments pass through an automatic filter before publication; comments found to break the rules are not published. Every user can report a comment and block a user; a blocked user's comments are not shown to the user who blocked them, and the blocked user cannot tag them. A comment reported by several users may be hidden until it is reviewed.

WinwayStats and volunteer moderators appointed by WinwayStats may hide comments that break the rules; WinwayStats may delete them. A User who breaks the rules may lose the right to comment temporarily or permanently; for serious or repeated violations, the provisions on suspending and closing the account apply. Hiding a comment or a commenting ban is notified to the User in the Application; the User may object through the feedback card in the Application or at info@winway.com.tr.

The User who writes a comment is responsible for its content; comments do not reflect the views of WinwayStats. The User grants WinwayStats a free licence, valid until the account is deleted, to show their comments to other users in the Application.

7. Virtual Coins and Points

Coins and points are entirely virtual and have no real-money value. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred, converted into money or inherited, and they do not constitute an asset owned by the User; they only allow use within the Application.

A confirmed coupon cannot be changed or deleted, and coupon history cannot be hidden. This rule is part of the Application's behavioural awareness purpose.

In the event of a technical error, a data provider error or a later correction of a result, WinwayStats may re-settle the affected coupons according to official results and correct coins and points accordingly.

A Personal Pisagol is a separate virtual account: it starts with 50,000 coins on the 1st of each month, does not take part in the player ranking or rewards, and its coins cannot be transferred to the User's account. The Personal Pisagol's predictions are shown only to the User; in the monthly Pisagol ranking, which has no rewards, only its aggregate results (number of predictions, number won and lost, return, net coins, points) are shown to other users together with the User's username. A Personal Pisagol only creates coupons with its own virtual balance according to the rules set by the User; it does not place coupons from the User's account. When the package period ends it creates no new predictions; its settings and history are kept. When the User's account is deleted, the Personal Pisagol and its coupons are deleted as well.

8. Match Data and Settlement

Match, odds and statistics data come from third-party data providers; delays, omissions or errors may occur.

Coupons are settled according to the official results reported by the data provider and the principles explained in the Rules section of the Application. If a match is postponed or cancelled, or the data needed for settlement cannot be obtained, the selection concerned is refunded.

Reward request records and delivery details are kept for 10 years from the delivery date as required by tax and commercial legislation; the delivery address and phone number are deleted once delivery is complete and the objection period has passed (3 months after delivery).

9. Rankings and Rewards

The ranking is determined by points only and resets on the 1st of each month. The monthly ranking is a skill competition based on the total result of prediction decisions over a month; the detailed rules are set out in the Competition and Reward Rules, which form an integral part of this Agreement (in the Application under Settings → Competition and Reward Rules; winwaystats.com/en/yarisma-kurallari).

From 1 December 2026, each month the Users ranked in the top 20 by points among those who meet the reward conditions receive in-app access to a Winway Plus package as a reward: places 1–13 receive 1 month of PRO, places 14–20 receive 15 days of PRO. For November 2026 and earlier months, the conditions in force in those months apply. The package reward is in-app package access only; it is not money, coins, points, goods or any other service.

Reward conditions (from 1 December 2026): a balance above 50,000 coins at the end of the month; at least 120 points that month; an account at least 30 days old at the end of the month; and coupons placed on at least 14 different days that month. Reward places are determined only among Users who meet these conditions; if points are equal, the User with the higher winning-coupon rate ranks higher, and if that is also equal, the one who comes first in username order. WinwayStats may change the conditions for future months and announces any change in the Application before it takes effect.

The reward is credited to the User's account automatically after all coupons of that month have been settled. For a User with an active package, the time won starts after the current package ends. The reward package is free; it cannot be converted into cash, coins or another reward, cannot be transferred to another account, and is not subject to refund or withdrawal. WinwayStats staff and test accounts do not receive rewards.

Spendable points and Reward Period: The points of months that meet the conditions are added to a spendable points balance separate from ranking points and can only be used for rewards such as gift vouchers and products during the Reward Period specified in the Competition and Reward Rules; they expire at season end (31 July). Money is not given as a reward; WinwayStats does not convert a reward into cash, coins or points. In the 2026-27 season, physical rewards are delivered only to delivery addresses in Türkiye. A User whose reward request is approved is asked, for delivery only, for name, surname, delivery address, phone number and a declaration of being over 18; the processing of this data is explained in the Privacy Policy.

Rewards cannot be bought with money. The Pisagol ranking has no rewards. If WinwayStats launches another reward programme in the future, its terms will be announced in a separate text before the programme starts.

Points and rankings obtained through cheating, multiple accounts, automation, account sharing or conduct contrary to this Agreement are void. Every reward request is reviewed before approval; a request with a violation is rejected and the related points are void.

The monthly ranking and rewards are organised and given solely by WinwayStats; participation is free. Each reward month is a calendar month (Türkiye time) and ends on the last day of that month. At most 20 Users win a package reward each month (10 before 1 December 2026); package rewards are credited no later than the 8th day of the following month.

No paid subscription or any other payment is required to take part in the competition, earn points or claim a reward by meeting the conditions. The membership type does not change the monthly coin budget, how coupons are scored, the ranking and tie-break rules or the reward conditions. A paid subscription does not by itself entitle anyone to a reward and does not guarantee winning one.

Basic ranking information (username, position, points and the summary values in the ranking table) and the User's own coupon history, points, position, balance and reward status can be viewed with every membership type. Access to other users' detailed coupon history and their pending coupons open to sharing may differ by membership package; the Packages screen shows which package includes it. The final ranking on which rewards are based and the information disclosed under the Competition and Reward Rules (e.g. the 20 users with priority claim rights) are shown to all users.

Apple Inc. and Google LLC are not sponsors of this ranking or its rewards and are in no way involved in them.

10. Paid Packages

The content, duration and price including taxes of each package are shown in the Application before purchase. Packages provide additional analysis tools and a loyalty badge.

Where the package description says so, a package also gives access to additional views such as other users' detailed coupon history and their pending coupons open to sharing. This access does not widen other users' privacy and sharing choices; it covers only the information that may be shown to other users under the Privacy Policy.

Purchases are made through an app store (Google Play, App Store) or a licensed payment institution contracted by WinwayStats; card details never reach WinwayStats. For purchases made through an app store, payment, refund and cancellation are also subject to that store's rules.

Packages are services performed instantly in electronic form. Under Article 15(1)(ğ) of the Turkish Distance Contracts Regulation, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised for such services once performance has started with the User's approval. The User is informed of this and their approval is obtained before purchase.

The Preliminary Information Form and the Distance Sales Contract are linked on the purchase screen and can be read in the app at any time. A User who completes a subscription purchase is deemed to have read and accepted both texts.

In the Android app, packages are offered as auto-renewing subscriptions through Google Play: at the end of the chosen period (monthly, 6-monthly or yearly) the subscription renews for the same period unless the User cancels it, and the fee is charged to the Google Play account. The User may cancel at any time in Google Play > Subscriptions; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period and the package stays active until then.

In the iPhone app, packages are offered as auto-renewing subscriptions through the App Store: at the end of the chosen period (monthly, 6-monthly or yearly) the subscription renews for the same period unless the User cancels it, and the fee is charged to the Apple account. The User may cancel at any time on iPhone in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period and the package stays active until then.

On Google Play, when the User changes package, the old package ends immediately and the value of its remaining time is added to the new package as time, according to Google Play's rules. On the App Store, Apple's rules apply: a move to a higher package starts immediately, and a move to a lower package starts at the next renewal. Only one package can be active on an account at a time.

The User is informed in advance of price changes in line with the app store's rules. RevenueCat Inc. is used to verify purchases and to pass renewal and cancellation information to WinwayStats.

New members receive an 11-day (264-hour) free trial. The trial does not turn into a paid subscription by itself; a User who does not buy a package at the end of the trial continues with free use. The trial may be used only once per person.

During the trial and for the duration of a package given as a promotion or reward, that package's access is open. When the period ends, the access of free use applies; this does not affect the User's own coupon history, coupons already played, points earned, ranking or reward rights.
