2. Definitions

• Coin: The virtual unit used to create coupons in the Application, credited to the account as 50,000 on the 1st of each month. It is not money or a right with monetary value.

• Points: The virtual measure calculated from coupon results that determines the ranking.

• Coupon: A virtual prediction record made up of match outcomes the User selects using coins.

• Pisagol: Sample predictions shared in the Application by WinwayStats's statistical model.

• Personal Pisagol: A tool that the User sets up with leagues, teams and rules of their own choice, whose predictions only the User can see, and that creates sample predictions in the Application with its own separate virtual coin balance.

• Package (Winway Plus): A paid membership giving access to additional analysis tools for a set period.

3. Nature of the Service

The Application is a behavioural awareness platform offering football statistics, predictions with virtual coins, rankings and analysis tools that let the User see their own decision behaviour.

No real-money betting, games of chance or gambling take place in the Application; no money is deposited and no money is won. Coins and points cannot be converted into money, goods or any other value.

Odds shown in the Application come from independent data providers and are used only to calculate virtual points. WinwayStats is not a representative or partner of any betting company and does not direct the User to any betting site.

Statistics, odds and predictions (including Pisagol) are for information only; they are not betting, investment or financial advice, and their accuracy is not guaranteed. The User is solely responsible for any actions they take outside the Application based on this information.

The Application does not provide psychological, medical or therapeutic services, does not diagnose and does not make decisions on the User's behalf. If the User feels in difficulty, they can contact the support organisations shown in the Application.

6. Community Rules and Comments

Users can comment on matches and on the statistics on the team statistics and team comparison pages, reply to comments, like coupons and comments, and tag users with whom they have a follow relationship in match comments using @username. A statistics comment is linked to a statistic chosen by the User and is shown together with that statistic's value at the time of writing; these comments are for observations based on statistics and may not contain tips, claims of certain outcomes or coupon sharing. Users cannot delete their comments; they may request deletion, which is reviewed by a moderator. Users are asked to accept these rules before their first comment.

The following are prohibited in comments: insults, swearing, hate speech and discrimination; harassment, threats and bullying; sexual content; spam, advertising and sharing links; sharing the User's own or another person's personal information (phone, e-mail, address, etc.); promoting or referring to betting and gambling sites; any unlawful content.

Comments pass through an automatic filter before publication; comments found to break the rules are not published. Every user can report a comment and block a user; a blocked user's comments are not shown to the user who blocked them, and the blocked user cannot tag them. A comment reported by several users may be hidden until it is reviewed.

WinwayStats and volunteer moderators appointed by WinwayStats may hide comments that break the rules; WinwayStats may delete them. A User who breaks the rules may lose the right to comment temporarily or permanently; for serious or repeated violations, the provisions on suspending and closing the account apply. Hiding a comment or a commenting ban is notified to the User in the Application; the User may object through the feedback card in the Application or at info@winway.com.tr.

The User who writes a comment is responsible for its content; comments do not reflect the views of WinwayStats. The User grants WinwayStats a free licence, valid until the account is deleted, to show their comments to other users in the Application.

7. Virtual Coins and Points

Coins and points are entirely virtual and have no real-money value. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred, converted into money or inherited, and they do not constitute an asset owned by the User; they only allow use within the Application.

A confirmed coupon cannot be changed or deleted, and coupon history cannot be hidden. This rule is part of the Application's behavioural awareness purpose.

In the event of a technical error, a data provider error or a later correction of a result, WinwayStats may re-settle the affected coupons according to official results and correct coins and points accordingly.

A Personal Pisagol is a separate virtual account: it starts with 50,000 coins on the 1st of each month, does not take part in the player ranking or rewards, and its coins cannot be transferred to the User's account. The Personal Pisagol's predictions are shown only to the User; in the monthly Pisagol ranking, which has no rewards, only its aggregate results (number of predictions, number won and lost, return, net coins, points) are shown to other users together with the User's username. A Personal Pisagol only creates coupons with its own virtual balance according to the rules set by the User; it does not place coupons from the User's account. When the package period ends it creates no new predictions; its settings and history are kept. When the User's account is deleted, the Personal Pisagol and its coupons are deleted as well.

8. Match Data and Settlement

Match, odds and statistics data come from third-party data providers; delays, omissions or errors may occur.

Coupons are settled according to the official results reported by the data provider and the principles explained in the Rules section of the Application. If a match is postponed or cancelled, or the data needed for settlement cannot be obtained, the selection concerned is refunded.

9. Rankings and Rewards

Rankings are determined solely by points and reset on the 1st of each month.

Each month, among Users who meet the reward conditions, those in the top 10 places by points receive in-app access to a Winway Plus package as a reward: places 1–7 receive 1 month of PRO, places 8–10 receive 7 days of STARTER. The reward is in-app package access only; it is not money, coins, points, goods or any other service.

Reward conditions: a balance above 50,000 coins at the end of the month; at least 170 points that month for places 1–5 and at least 100 points for places 6–10; an account at least 30 days old at the end of the month (at least 7 days for October 2026); and coupons placed on at least 10 different days that month. Reward places are determined only among Users who meet these conditions; if points are equal, the User with the higher winning-coupon rate ranks higher, and if that is also equal, the one who comes first in username order. The current conditions are shown in the Rules section of the Application; WinwayStats may change the conditions for future months and announces any change in the Application.

The reward is added to the User's account automatically once all coupons of that month have been settled. If the User already has an active package, the time won starts after the current package ends. The reward package is free of charge; it cannot be exchanged for cash, coins or another reward, cannot be transferred to another account, and is not subject to refunds or the right of withdrawal. WinwayStats's staff and test accounts do not receive rewards.

Rewards cannot be bought with money; paid packages give no advantage in the ranking or in winning rewards. The Pisagol ranking has no rewards. If WinwayStats launches physical rewards or another reward programme in the future, the type of rewards, eligibility conditions and delivery terms will be announced in a separate text before the programme starts.

Points and rankings obtained through cheating, multiple accounts or conduct contrary to this Agreement may be declared invalid; any reward won this way may be withdrawn.

The content, duration and price including taxes of each package are shown in the Application before purchase. Packages only provide additional analysis tools and a loyalty badge; they give no advantage in coins, points, rankings or rewards.

Purchases are made through an app store (Google Play, App Store) or a licensed payment institution contracted by WinwayStats; card details never reach WinwayStats. For purchases made through an app store, payment, refund and cancellation are also subject to that store's rules.

Packages are services performed instantly in electronic form. Under Article 15(1)(ğ) of the Turkish Distance Contracts Regulation, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised for such services once performance has started with the User's approval. The User is informed of this and their approval is obtained before purchase.

The Preliminary Information Form and the Distance Sales Contract are linked on the purchase screen and can be read in the app at any time. A User who completes a subscription purchase is deemed to have read and accepted both texts.

In the Android app, packages are offered as auto-renewing subscriptions through Google Play: at the end of the chosen period (weekly, monthly, 6-monthly or yearly) the subscription renews for the same period unless the User cancels it, and the fee is charged to the Google Play account. The User may cancel at any time in Google Play > Subscriptions; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period and the package stays active until then.

When the User changes package, the old package ends immediately and the value of its remaining time is added to the new package as time, according to Google Play's rules. Only one package can be active on an account at a time.

The User is informed in advance of price changes in line with the app store's rules. RevenueCat Inc. is used to verify purchases and to pass renewal and cancellation information to WinwayStats.

New members receive a 48-hour free trial. The trial does not turn into a paid subscription by itself; a User who does not buy a package at the end of the trial continues with free use. The trial may be used only once per person.
