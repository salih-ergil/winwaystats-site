TRENDERUES

Get to know your decisions through data.

WinwayStats is a behavioural awareness app that offers the football prediction experience without real money. You start every month with 50,000 virtual coins; coins cannot be bought, sold or converted into money.

No Real MoneyPermanent HistoryAwareness Through Data

Virtual budget

50,000 coins on the 1st of every month. Unused coins do not carry over; every month starts fresh.

Permanent history

A confirmed slip cannot be changed and losses cannot be hidden.

Your own data

See your budget, decisions and habits without labels or judgement.

Statistics

Real matches, live scores, team statistics and head-to-head records.

The app is coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

WinwayStats is not a betting app. There are no deposits, no withdrawals and no real-money play. The app is for users aged 18 and over.

If you would like support, the helpline for your country is shown in the app.
